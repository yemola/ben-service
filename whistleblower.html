Whistle Blowing

Norrenberger Pensions Limited will not ask for gratification in any form, either monetary or material before or after processing your withdrawal application. If you suspect any corruption, malpractice or unethical behaviour, you can report it through the channels below.

How to report

Reports are taken seriously. You may report anonymously, but providing contact details will help us investigate and follow up where necessary.

  • By email
    Send details to: clientservice@norrenpensions.com
  • By telephone
    Call our client service line (see the Reach Us page for phone numbers).
  • Online form
    You may submit a completed report using our Whistle Blowing form (if available) — include dates, names, locations and any supporting evidence.

What we will not accept

We will not tolerate demands for gratification or payments related to withdrawal processing or other services. Any staff found soliciting or accepting gratification will be subject to investigation and disciplinary action.

What happens next?

All reports are reviewed and investigated by our compliance team. We treat whistle-blowing reports confidentially and will take action where appropriate to protect stakeholders and our business integrity.

Report now

If you'd like to submit a written report now, use the form below. Provide as much detail as possible. You may remain anonymous.
